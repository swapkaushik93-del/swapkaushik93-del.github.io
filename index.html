02 / How I think

Curiosity, with a practical bias.

I want to understand the problem before choosing a tool, make assumptions visible and learn from small tests before investing in a big solution.

01 — Notice

Start with people.

Look for friction in real situations and understand who experiences it, how often and why it matters.

02 — Test

Make the smallest useful version.

Use public information, transparent logic and quick experiments to find out whether an idea helps.

03 — Learn

Earn the next step.

Share what works, be honest about what doesn’t, and let evidence—not novelty—guide the next iteration.
